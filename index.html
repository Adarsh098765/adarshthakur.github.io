About

An engineer who owns the whole feature, from Figma to Crashlytics.

I'm an Android engineer with 5 years of experience on consumer apps with large and varied user bases: expectant parents, couples planning weddings, and delivery riders. My work is usually two things: shipping revenue-critical features such as cart, checkout, payments and onboarding, and making the app faster and harder to crash.

I care about architecture that a team can grow on (MVVM / Clean Architecture, Hilt, Coroutines & Flow), migrating legacy code without breaking production (Java → Kotlin, XML → Compose), and backing changes with data: A/B gates, analytics events, crash and ANR dashboards.

I also use AI-assisted development (Claude Code, Firebase MCP) every day to move faster on refactors, code review and crash triage.

What I bring to a team

  • End-to-end feature ownership in Jetpack Compose
  • Crash, ANR, memory-leak and startup-time work
  • Legacy migrations: Java→Kotlin, XML→Compose, Volley→Retrofit
  • Payments, checkout, NUX/onboarding and A/B-tested funnels
  • Real-time systems: Socket.IO chat, FCM, WorkManager
  • Localization for Hindi and other Indic languages
